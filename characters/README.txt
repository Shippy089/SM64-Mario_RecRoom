Super Luigi 64 v2.0.zip is Super Luigi 64 by Cjes and CyberTanuki, unchanged from their release (its own readme and
changelog are inside). Special thanks from them to Dudaw, Flotonic and Subdrag. Website: True64.webs.com

Luigi belongs to Nintendo. This is their patch, not a ROM: the launcher applies it in memory to your own Super Mario 64
ROM when you pick Character: Luigi. Nothing patched is written to disk.
