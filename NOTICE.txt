What is in this package
  - The launcher (dist\MarioLauncher.*), the Mario mod for the BepInEx loader (dist\plugins) and route files (routes\).
  - dist\native\sm64-mario.dll: a build of libsm64 (https://github.com/libsm64/libsm64, commit
    fd11813208272b4271d92bd92feb8f3fdbe61be5, built with zig; the recipe is in native\). libsm64 reimplements Super Mario 64's
    movement and physics from the community decompilation (https://github.com/n64decomp/sm64). Read the licence and credits of
    those projects before you pass the package on. The library contains no copy of the Super Mario 64 ROM: textures and sounds are
    read from your own ROM when the game starts.
  - BepInEx-pack\: the BepInEx 6.0.0-be.788 IL2CPP win-x64 mod loader, unmodified, as published at builds.bepinex.dev
    (https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx). Its licences and the sources of its parts are listed in BepInEx-pack\README.txt.

What is NOT in this package, and what you supply
  - Rec Room: an old IL2CPP client build (2019 to April 8, 2020) is yours to download with your own Steam account.
  - Super Mario 64: your own cartridge dump (USA, .z64). It is read on your computer and never copied or sent anywhere.

Rec Room and Super Mario 64 belong to their owners. Check the terms of the games before you share recordings or builds.
